Common questions

Before you ask…

Quick answers, sorted by what you're planning.

How early should I enquire for a festival?

As early as you can. Share your date, assortment and quantity, and the store will confirm what's possible.

Can I customize a gift box?

Tell us your products, occasion and presentation ideas in the form. The store confirms which combinations are available.

Who do I contact about a bulk order?

Choose "Bulk & corporate orders" as your topic and include the event date, estimated quantity and packing needs.

Where can I find ingredient and storage details?

Check the product label and product page. If anything is unclear, ask us before purchasing or consuming.

Can I visit without an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome during open hours. For a large requirement, a quick message helps the store prepare.

Can I send a gift to someone else?

Mention it in your message. Delivery and gifting arrangements are confirmed with the store.
