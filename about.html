Our story

A shop built around shared moments.

Mithai Tales began with a simple idea: the best sweets are the ones that bring people closer. A box passed across a table, a plate offered to a guest, a treat saved for tea time.

Today we bring that thinking to every assortment: familiar recipes, honest flavors and presentation that feels ready to give.

Every bite tells a sweet story. We simply help you share it.
