Talk to Us
What should I include in my festival enquiry?

Share the occasion, your preferred date, approximate quantity and the sweets, savories or gift boxes you like. Estimates are fine, and the store will help refine them.

How early should I plan?

Planning ahead gives the best chance of confirming your preferred selection. Enquire as early as you can and confirm availability with the store before finalizing your plans.

Can I mix sweets and savories in one order?

Yes, sweet and savory combinations are a popular way to build a festival assortment. Share your preferences and the store can confirm which combinations are available.

Can the boxes be customized?

Describe your idea through the Contact page. The store will confirm what customization and presentation options are possible.

Where can I find ingredient and storage details?

Follow the product label for ingredients, allergens, storage and best-before guidance. If anything is unclear, ask through the Contact page before ordering.
