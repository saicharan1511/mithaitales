Our story & craft

Rooted in Tradition.
Made to Share.

There is comfort in a familiar sweet: the texture, the aroma, and the moment it brings to mind. Our approach begins with those simple pleasures.

From selecting ingredients to preparing and arranging each assortment, care belongs in every step. Traditional recipes guide a collection made for family tables, thoughtful visits, and celebrations big and small.

  • Ingredients with purpose

    Flavors and textures chosen to suit each recipe.

  • Attention to preparation

    Care at the mixing, cooking and finishing stages.

  • Considered presentation

    Thoughtful arrangements that feel ready to share.

A little help choosing

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Start here, or visit our Contact page for details about your order.

What can I find in your collection?

Explore Sweets, Savories, Dry Fruits and Gift Boxes. Individual product pages provide the details for each selection and available pack options.

Can I customize a gift box?

Share your preferred products, occasion, quantity and presentation ideas through the Contact page. The store can confirm which combinations and customization options are available.

How should I plan a festival order?

Visit Festival Orders for inspiration, then enquire in advance with your preferred date, assortment and quantity. Confirm availability and arrangements before finalizing your plans.

Where can I find storage and ingredient information?

Follow the product label and product-specific information for ingredients, allergens, storage and best-before guidance. If anything is unclear, ask through the Contact page before purchasing or consuming.

Who should I contact about a bulk order?

Use the Contact page and include your event date, estimated quantity, product preferences and packing requirements. The store will need to confirm the available options.
